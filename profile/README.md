# RegTech Engineering Lab

Production grade reference systems for compliance automation, governed AI and financial crime operations.

RegTech Engineering Lab is an open-source engineering portfolio focused on the systems behind modern Compliance operations.

The projects demonstrate how regulatory requirements can be translated into secure, scalable and auditable software controls across

- AML and fraud monitoring
- AI governance and model-risk management
- KYC and third-party screening
- Regulatory change management
- Human-review and investigation workflows
- Data quality, lineage and regulatory evidence

All customer, merchant and transaction data used in these repositories is synthetic.
